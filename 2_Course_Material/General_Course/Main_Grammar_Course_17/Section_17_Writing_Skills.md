# Section 17: राइटिंग स्किल्स (Writing Skills & AI)

**🎯 इस सेक्शन का लक्ष्य:** कागज़, ईमेल या कंप्यूटर पर अपने विचार औपचारिक (Formal) तरीके से लिखना सीखना, और आज के समय की सबसे बड़ी ज़रूरत—'AI से बात करना' (Prompt Engineering) सीखना।

पिछले सेक्शन में हमने बोलने की खूब प्रैक्टिस की। अब हम लिखने पर ध्यान देंगे। बोलना जितना ज़रूरी है, सही तरीके से लिखना भी उतना ही ज़रूरी है, खास तौर पर ऑफिस या स्कूल/कॉलेज में।

## 1. Formal Letters (औपचारिक पत्र)

जब हम किसी अधिकारी, प्रिंसिपल, या कंपनी को पत्र लिखते हैं, तो उसे Formal Letter कहते हैं।

**Format (प्रारूप):**
1. Sender's Address (भेजने वाले का पता)
2. Date (तारीख - 15 August 2024)
3. Receiver's Address (प्राप्त करने वाले का पता)
4. Subject (विषय - पत्र किस बारे में है)
5. Salutation (Respected Sir / Madam)
6. Body of the Letter (मुख्य बात - 2/3 पैराग्राफ में)
7. Complimentary Close (Yours faithfully / sincerely)
8. Signature / Name (आपका नाम)

### Example 1: Letter to the Principal for 3 days sick leave.
**To,**
The Principal,
DAV Public School, Delhi.
**Date:** 15 October 2023
**Subject:** Application for sick leave.

**Respected Sir,**
I beg to say that I am a student of class 10th in your school. I have been suffering from a high fever since last night. The doctor has advised me to take rest for three days.
Therefore, I request you to kindly grant me leave for three days from 15th Oct to 17th Oct. I shall be highly obliged to you.

**Yours obediently,**
Rahul Kumar
Class: 10-A, Roll No: 15

### Example 2: Letter to the Bank Manager to issue a new ATM card.
**To,**
The Branch Manager,
State Bank of India, CP Branch, Delhi.
**Date:** 20 November 2023
**Subject:** Request for a new ATM card.

**Respected Sir,**
I have a savings account in your branch (A/c No: 1234567890). My old ATM card has expired this month. I urgently need a new ATM card for my daily transactions.
Kindly issue a new ATM card to my registered address as soon as possible. I have attached a copy of my Aadhar card for verification.

**Yours faithfully,**
Amit Singh
Mobile: 9876543210

### Example 3: Complaint letter to the Municipal Corporation about bad roads.
*(Format same as above...)*
**Subject:** Complaint regarding the poor condition of roads in Sector 4.
**Respected Sir,**
I am a resident of Sector 4. I want to draw your attention to the bad condition of the roads in our area. There are many potholes, which cause accidents every day. During the rainy season, the situation becomes worse.
I request you to take immediate action and repair the roads soon.
**Yours faithfully,**
Aman Verma

## 2. Notice Writing (सूचना लिखना)

Notice हमेशा एक **Box (बक्से)** के अंदर लिखा जाता है। यह कम से कम शब्दों में जानकारी देने के लिए होता है।

**Format:**
1. Name of the Institution/School
2. Word "NOTICE" (Capital letters)
3. Date
4. Heading / Title
5. Body (Date, Time, Venue - क्या, कब, कहाँ)
6. Name & Designation (लिखने वाले का नाम और पद)

Anyone who finds it is requested to return it to the undersigned or submit it to the Principal's office. A suitable reward will be given.
**Rohan Das**
(Class 9-B)

## 3. Essay Writing (निबंध लेखन)

Essay को 3 हिस्सों में बाँटा जाता है:
1. **Introduction (प्रस्तावना):** विषय के बारे में शुरुआत (2-3 लाइन)।
2. **Body (मुख्य भाग):** विषय के फायदे, नुकसान, कारण आदि (2-3 पैराग्राफ)।
3. **Conclusion (निष्कर्ष):** आपकी राय और अंत (2-3 लाइन)।

### 5 Essay Topics for Practice:
1. **My Best Friend:** (Introduction ➡️ Qualities ➡️ Why I like him/her ➡️ Conclusion).
2. **The Value of Trees / Environment:** (Introduction ➡️ Importance of oxygen/rain ➡️ Deforestation problems ➡️ Conclusion: Plant more trees).
3. **Wonders of Science:** (Introduction ➡️ Inventions like electricity, computers ➡️ Medical science ➡️ Disadvantages/Misuse ➡️ Conclusion).
4. **My Favorite Festival (Diwali):** (Introduction ➡️ Why it is celebrated ➡️ How we celebrate (lights, sweets) ➡️ Message of the festival).
5. **The Importance of Education:** (Introduction ➡️ How it changes life ➡️ Career & Society ➡️ Conclusion: Right to education).

## 4. AI Prompt Engineering (AI से कैसे बात करें?)

आज के समय में ChatGPT, Gemini जैसे AI टूल्स का इस्तेमाल सबको आना चाहिए। AI को जो निर्देश (Command) हम देते हैं, उसे **'Prompt'** कहते हैं।

**एक अच्छा Prompt कैसे लिखें? (ACT Formula)**
* **A - Action:** उसे बताएँ कि क्या करना है (Write, Translate, Explain).
* **C - Context:** उसे थोड़ी जानकारी दें (I am a student, Make it for kids).
* **T - Tone:** लहज़ा कैसा हो (Make it funny, Formal, Simple English).

**Examples of Good Prompts:**
* ❌ *Bad:* Write a letter for a job.
* ✅ *Good:* "Act as a software engineer. Write a formal email to an HR manager applying for the position of Web Developer. Keep the tone professional and the length short."
* ❌ *Bad:* Explain Newton's law.
* ✅ *Good:* "Explain Newton's third law of motion in very simple English, with 3 real-life examples. Explain it as if you are teaching a 10-year-old kid."
