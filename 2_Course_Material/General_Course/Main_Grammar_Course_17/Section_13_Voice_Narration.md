# Section 13: वाक्यों को घुमाना (Voice & Narration)

**🎯 इस सेक्शन का लक्ष्य:** एक ही बात को अलग-अलग तरीकों से कहना सीखना। यह एडवांस अंग्रेज़ी (Advanced English) का सबसे ज़रूरी हिस्सा है, जिसका इस्तेमाल अखबारों (News) और कानूनी भाषा (Law) में सबसे ज़्यादा होता है।

पिछले सेक्शन (Syntax) में हमने ग्रामर के पक्के नियमों को समझा। अब हम सीखेंगे कि अपनी बात को सीधे तरीके से और घुमाकर कैसे कहा जाता है।

---

## 1. Active and Passive Voice (वाच्य)

* **Active Voice (सीधी बात):** इसमें 'काम करने वाला' (Subject/हीरो) सबसे आगे होता है।
  * *राम ने रावण को मारा।* (Ram killed Ravan.)
* **Passive Voice (घुमावदार बात):** इसमें 'जिस पर काम हुआ' (Object) उसे सबसे आगे कर दिया जाता है। यह तब इस्तेमाल होता है जब काम करने वाले से ज़्यादा 'काम' अहम हो।
  * *रावण राम के द्वारा मारा गया।* (Ravan was killed by Ram.)

### Passive Voice का सबसे बड़ा नियम:
1. Object को उठाकर Subject की जगह रखें।
2. Tense के हिसाब से सही Helping Verb (is/was/be/been/being) लगाएँ।
3. **हमेशा Verb की 3rd Form (V3)** का इस्तेमाल करें। (Passive = V3 के बिना अधूरा है!)
4. आखिर में 'by + Subject' लगाएँ।

### 30 Active-Passive रूपांतरण (Examples)

**A. Present Indefinite (V1 ➡️ is/am/are + V3)**
1. **Active:** I write a letter. ➡️ **Passive:** A letter is written by me.
2. **Active:** She cooks food. ➡️ **Passive:** Food is cooked by her.
3. **Active:** They play cricket. ➡️ **Passive:** Cricket is played by them.
4. **Active:** He helps me. ➡️ **Passive:** I am helped by him.
5. **Active:** The police arrest thieves. ➡️ **Passive:** Thieves are arrested by the police.

**B. Present Continuous (is/am/are + V4 ➡️ is/am/are + being + V3)**
6. **Active:** She is singing a song. ➡️ **Passive:** A song is being sung by her.
7. **Active:** They are watching a movie. ➡️ **Passive:** A movie is being watched by them.
8. **Active:** I am reading a book. ➡️ **Passive:** A book is being read by me.
9. **Active:** The doctor is treating the patient. ➡️ **Passive:** The patient is being treated by the doctor.
10. **Active:** You are wasting time. ➡️ **Passive:** Time is being wasted by you.

**C. Present Perfect (has/have + V3 ➡️ has/have + been + V3)**
11. **Active:** I have finished the work. ➡️ **Passive:** The work has been finished by me.
12. **Active:** She has bought a car. ➡️ **Passive:** A car has been bought by her.
13. **Active:** The judge has given the verdict. ➡️ **Passive:** The verdict has been given by the judge.
14. **Active:** They have won the match. ➡️ **Passive:** The match has been won by them.
15. **Active:** He has broken the window. ➡️ **Passive:** The window has been broken by him.

**D. Past Indefinite (V2 ➡️ was/were + V3)**
16. **Active:** Ram killed Ravan. ➡️ **Passive:** Ravan was killed by Ram.
17. **Active:** The dog bit the man. ➡️ **Passive:** The man was bitten by the dog.
18. **Active:** She wrote a poem. ➡️ **Passive:** A poem was written by her.
19. **Active:** Columbus discovered America. ➡️ **Passive:** America was discovered by Columbus.
20. **Active:** I did not see him. ➡️ **Passive:** He was not seen by me.

**E. Past Continuous (was/were + V4 ➡️ was/were + being + V3)**
21. **Active:** He was writing a letter. ➡️ **Passive:** A letter was being written by him.
22. **Active:** They were building a house. ➡️ **Passive:** A house was being built by them.

**F. Past Perfect (had + V3 ➡️ had + been + V3)**
23. **Active:** I had completed my homework. ➡️ **Passive:** My homework had been completed by me.
24. **Active:** She had cooked dinner. ➡️ **Passive:** Dinner had been cooked by her.

**G. Future Indefinite (will + V1 ➡️ will be + V3)**
25. **Active:** I will buy a laptop. ➡️ **Passive:** A laptop will be bought by me.
26. **Active:** The police will arrest the criminal. ➡️ **Passive:** The criminal will be arrested by the police.
27. **Active:** She will write a story. ➡️ **Passive:** A story will be written by her.

**H. Modals (Modal + V1 ➡️ Modal + be + V3)**
28. **Active:** I can lift this box. ➡️ **Passive:** This box can be lifted by me.
29. **Active:** You should obey your parents. ➡️ **Passive:** Your parents should be obeyed by you.
30. **Active:** We must respect the law. ➡️ **Passive:** The law must be respected by us.

## 2. Direct and Indirect Speech (Narration)

Narration का मतलब है "किसी की कही हुई बात को बताना।" इसे दो तरीकों से बताया जा सकता है:
1. **Direct Speech:** किसी की बात को हुबहू (exactly) उसके ही शब्दों में बताना (Double Quotes " " के अंदर)।
   * *Ram said, "I am busy."* (राम ने कहा, "मैं व्यस्त हूँ।")
2. **Indirect Speech:** किसी की बात को अपने शब्दों में बदलकर बताना। 
   * *Ram said that he was busy.* (राम ने कहा कि वह व्यस्त था।)

### Narration बदलने के 3 सुनहरे नियम (SON Formula)
**S.O.N = Subject, Object, No Change**
* **First Person (I, We):** वाक्य के **S**ubject के अनुसार बदलता है।
* **Second Person (You):** वाक्य के **O**bject के अनुसार बदलता है।
* **Third Person (He, She, They):** **N**o Change (नहीं बदलता)।

*अगर बात Past में कही गई थी (said), तो Tense भी Past में बदल जाता है (is ➡️ was, have ➡️ had)।*

### 30 Narration रूपांतरण (Examples)

**A. Simple Statements (साधारण वाक्य)**
1. **Direct:** He said, "I am happy." ➡️ **Indirect:** He said that he was happy.
2. **Direct:** Ram said, "I like apples." ➡️ **Indirect:** Ram said that he liked apples.
3. **Direct:** Sita said to me, "You are late." ➡️ **Indirect:** Sita told me that I was late. *(said to ➡️ told)*
4. **Direct:** They said, "We have won the match." ➡️ **Indirect:** They said that they had won the match.
5. **Direct:** She said, "I will go to Delhi." ➡️ **Indirect:** She said that she would go to Delhi. *(will ➡️ would)*
6. **Direct:** He said to her, "I can help you." ➡️ **Indirect:** He told her that he could help her. *(can ➡️ could)*
7. **Direct:** The teacher said, "The earth is round." ➡️ **Indirect:** The teacher said that the earth is round. *(Universal Truth में Tense नहीं बदलता)*
8. **Direct:** My friend said, "I am reading a book." ➡️ **Indirect:** My friend said that he was reading a book.
9. **Direct:** He said, "I bought a car yesterday." ➡️ **Indirect:** He said that he had bought a car the previous day. *(yesterday ➡️ the previous day)*
10. **Direct:** She said, "I will do it tomorrow." ➡️ **Indirect:** She said that she would do it the next day. *(tomorrow ➡️ the next day)*

**B. Interrogative (प्रश्नवाचक - पूछना)**
*(इनमें 'said' को 'asked' में बदलते हैं, और that की जगह if/whether लगाते हैं)*
11. **Direct:** He said to me, "Are you okay?" ➡️ **Indirect:** He asked me if I was okay.
12. **Direct:** She said to him, "Do you know me?" ➡️ **Indirect:** She asked him if he knew her.
13. **Direct:** Ram said to Sita, "Will you come?" ➡️ **Indirect:** Ram asked Sita if she would come.
14. **Direct:** He said to me, "What is your name?" ➡️ **Indirect:** He asked me what my name was. *(W-H words में if नहीं लगता)*
15. **Direct:** She said, "Where do you live?" ➡️ **Indirect:** She asked where I lived.
16. **Direct:** The police said to the thief, "Why did you steal?" ➡️ **Indirect:** The police asked the thief why he had stolen.
17. **Direct:** I said to him, "Have you finished?" ➡️ **Indirect:** I asked him if he had finished.
18. **Direct:** He asked, "Who is there?" ➡️ **Indirect:** He asked who was there.
19. **Direct:** She said to me, "When will the train arrive?" ➡️ **Indirect:** She asked me when the train would arrive.
20. **Direct:** My father said, "Did you study?" ➡️ **Indirect:** My father asked if I had studied.

**C. Imperative (आदेश या निवेदन)**
*(इनमें 'said' को ordered/requested/advised में बदलते हैं, और वाक्यों को 'to' से जोड़ते हैं)*
21. **Direct:** The teacher said to the boys, "Sit down." ➡️ **Indirect:** The teacher ordered the boys to sit down.
22. **Direct:** He said to me, "Please help me." ➡️ **Indirect:** He requested me to help him.
23. **Direct:** The doctor said to the patient, "Take rest." ➡️ **Indirect:** The doctor advised the patient to take rest.
24. **Direct:** The officer said, "Shoot the enemy!" ➡️ **Indirect:** The officer ordered to shoot the enemy.
25. **Direct:** My mother said, "Do not go out in the sun." ➡️ **Indirect:** My mother forbade me to go out in the sun. *(forbade = मना करना)*

**D. Exclamatory & Optative (हैरानी और दुआ)**
26. **Direct:** He said, "Alas! I am ruined." ➡️ **Indirect:** He exclaimed with sorrow that he was ruined.
27. **Direct:** They said, "Hurrah! We won." ➡️ **Indirect:** They exclaimed with joy that they had won.
28. **Direct:** The old man said, "May God bless you." ➡️ **Indirect:** The old man prayed that God might bless me.
29. **Direct:** She said, "Wow! What a beautiful flower." ➡️ **Indirect:** She exclaimed with wonder that it was a very beautiful flower.
30. **Direct:** He said, "Good morning, sir." ➡️ **Indirect:** He respectfully wished his sir good morning.
