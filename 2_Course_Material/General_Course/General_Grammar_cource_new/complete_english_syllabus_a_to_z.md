# 📚 A to Z Master English Syllabus (Basic to Advanced)

यहाँ पुराने ग्रामर कोर्स (182 आर्टिकल्स) और नए किड्स कोर्स को मिलाकर एक **Master Sequence** तैयार किया गया है। इसमें ज़ीरो (A,B,C,D) से लेकर एडवांस कॉम्पिटिटिव एग्जाम्स (SSC/Bank) और स्पोकन इंग्लिश तक का **हर एक टॉपिक, सब-टॉपिक और टर्म** शामिल है। कोई भी हिस्सा छोड़ा नहीं गया है।

> **नोट:** यहाँ केवल "Terms" (नाम) दिए गए हैं, डेफिनिशन नहीं। इसे 8वीं क्लास के बच्चे से लेकर किसी बड़े को भी इसी क्रम (Sequence) में पढ़ाया जाना चाहिए, ताकि हर अगला टॉपिक पिछले टॉपिक से जुड़ा रहे।

## 🟢 1. शुरुआत: भाषा, अक्षर और उच्चारण (Basics & Phonics)
* **Language & Communication:** SVO vs SOV Pattern, World Languages.
* **Alphabet (वर्णमाला):** Capital (A-Z) & Small (a-z) letters.
* **Bridge (हिंदी से अंग्रेज़ी):** Vowels (a, aa, i, ee...), Consonants (k, kh, g...).
* **Barahkhadi & Name Writing:** (ka, kaa, ki, kee...), Writing personal/place names.
* **Sounds (Phonics):** 26 Letters vs 44 Sounds.
  * **Vowel Sounds:** Short vowels, Long vowels, Diphthongs.
  * **Consonant Sounds:** Voiced vs Voiceless.
  * **Categories of Sounds:** Plosives (P,B,T,D,K,G), Fricatives (F,V,S,Z), Nasals (M,N,NG), Approximants (L,R,W,Y).
* **Pronunciation Nuances:**
  * Hindi-English Comparison (Th vs थ, V vs W, F vs Ph).
  * Syllables & Word Stress.
  * The Schwa Sound (/ə/).
  * Silent Letters (K in Knife, L in Walk, W in Write).
  * Spelling Rules (Magic E - Tap vs Tape).

## 🟡 2. बुनियादी शब्द और वाक्य के हिस्से (Building Blocks)
* **First Vocabulary:** Body parts, Colors, Shapes, Fruits, Vegetables, Animals, Classroom items, Home items, Time, Numbers.
* **Basic Units of Language:** Word, Phrase, Clause, Sentence.
* **Parts of Speech (Overview):** Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction, Interjection.
* **Sentence Structure:** Subject and Predicate.
* **Objects:** Direct Object, Indirect Object, Retained Object, Cognate Object.
* **Complements:** Subject Complement, Object Complement.

## 🟠 3. संज्ञा (Noun) - नाम वाले शब्द
* **Classification:** Proper Noun, Common Noun, Collective Noun, Material Noun, Abstract Noun.
* **Countability:** Countable Nouns, Uncountable Nouns.
* **Number (Singular/Plural):** 
  * Regular Plurals, Irregular Plurals (Child/Children).
  * Foreign Plurals (Datum/Data).
  * Always Singular Nouns (News, Furniture), Always Plural Nouns (Scissors, Police).
* **Gender:** Masculine, Feminine, Common, Neuter.
* **Case:** 
  * Possessive Case (Apostrophe 's rule).
  * Personification of Nouns.
  * Objective Case Triggers.

## 🟠 4. सर्वनाम (Pronoun) - नाम की जगह
* **Personal Pronouns:** First, Second, Third Person (I, You, He, She, It, We, They).
* **Ordering Rules:** 231 Rule, 123 Rule (You, He, and I).
* **Possessive Pronouns:** Mine, Yours, His, Hers, Ours, Theirs.
* **Reflexive & Emphatic Pronouns:** Myself, Yourself, Himself.
  * Verbs that Prohibit Reflexive Pronouns.
* **Demonstrative Pronouns:** This, That, These, Those.
* **Reciprocal Pronouns:** Each other, One another.
* **Distributive Pronouns:** Each, Either, Neither.
* **Indefinite Pronouns:** Someone, Anybody, Nothing, All, Some.
* **Relative Pronouns:** Who, Whom, Whose, Which, That.

## 🔵 5. विशेषण और डिटरमिनर्स (Adjectives & Determiners)
* **Classification:** Qualitative, Quantitative, Demonstrative, Possessive, Interrogative.
* **Degrees of Comparison:** Positive, Comparative, Superlative.
* **Order of Adjectives:** OSASCOMP rule (Opinion, Size, Age, Shape, Color, Origin, Material, Purpose).
* **Specific Adjectives:** Numerals, Quantifiers (Much, Many, Few, Little).
* **Advanced Adjective Rules:** Attributive vs Absolute Adjectives, Latin Adjectives.
* **Determiners vs Adjectives.**
* **Articles (A, An, The):**
  * A/An (Vowel vs Consonant Sound Logic).
  * The (Geographical use, Class representation).
  * Zero Article (Omission of Articles).
  * Primary vs Secondary Purpose (School vs The School).
  * Repetition of Articles (A black and white dog vs A black and a white dog).

## 🟣 6. क्रिया और स्थिति (Verbs, Status & Possession)
* **Types of Verbs:** Action Verbs vs Stative (State) Verbs, Transitive vs Intransitive Verbs, Linking / Copular Verbs.
* **Verb Forms:** V1, V2, V3, V4, V5 (Weak Verbs vs Strong Verbs).
* **Status (To Be):** Present (Is/Am/Are), Past (Was/Were), Future (Will be).
* **Possession (To Have):** Present (Has/Have), Past (Had).
* **Dummy Subjects:** Introductory "It" (Time, Weather), Introductory "There" (Presence).
* **Planned & Compulsive Actions:** Is to/Am to, Has to/Have to/Had to.
* **Emphatic Sentences:** Do/Does/Did for emphasis.
* **Causative Verbs:** Get, Make, Have, Let, Help (काम करवाना).
* **Non-Finite Verbs:** Infinitives (To + V1), Bare Infinitives.
* **Gerunds:** V4 acting as Noun.
* **Participles:** Present, Past, Perfect Participles.
* **Mood in English Grammar:** Indicative, Imperative, Subjunctive.

## 🟤 7. समय का चक्र (Tenses)
* **Tense Introduction:** Time vs Tense.
* **Present Tense:** 
  * Simple Present (Habits, Universal truths, Future commentary).
  * Present Continuous (Ongoing actions).
  * Present Perfect (Completed actions with present effect).
  * Present Perfect Continuous (Ongoing since a past time).
* **Past Tense:** 
  * Simple Past (Finished actions).
  * Past Continuous (Actions ongoing in past).
  * Past Perfect (Action completed before another past action).
  * Past Perfect Continuous.
* **Future Tense:** Future Simple, Future Continuous, Future Perfect, Future Perfect Continuous.
* **Advanced Tense Rules:** 
  * Present Perfect vs Simple Past.
  * "It is high time" Past Rule.

## 🔴 8. मॉडल्स (Modals)
* **Ability, Permission, Possibility:** Can, Could, May, Might.
* **Advice, Obligation, Duty:** Should, Must, Ought to.
* **Future & Politeness:** Will, Would.
* **Semi-Modals / Marginals:** Need, Dare, Used to.
* **Perfect Modals:** Should have, Could have, Would have, Must have.

## 🟠 9. क्रिया विशेषण (Adverb)
* **Kinds of Adverbs:** Time, Place, Manner, Frequency, Degree.
* **Order of Adverbs:** MPFT Rule (Manner, Place, Frequency, Time).
* **Specific Adverbs Use:** Enough, Only.
* **Inversion of Adverbs:** (Hardly had I..., No sooner did he...).
* **Confusing Adverbs:** Hard vs Hardly, Late vs Lately.

## 🟡 10. संबंधबोधक (Preposition)
* **Categories:** Place (At, In, On), Time (Since, For, From), Movement (Into, Through, Across, Along).
* **Confusing Prepositions:** Beside vs Besides, Between vs Among.
* **Fixed Prepositions:** Depend on, Consist of, Fond of.
* **Omission of Prepositions:** Where NOT to use them.

## 🟢 11. समुच्चयबोधक (Conjunction)
* **Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS):** For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So.
* **Subordinating Conjunctions:** Because, Although, When, If.
* **Correlative Conjunctions:** Either...or, Neither...nor, Not only...but also.
* **Conjunction Traps / Common Errors.**

## 🔵 12. वाक्य, क्लॉज़ और सिंटेक्स (Sentences, Clauses & Syntax)
* **Types of Sentences (Meaning):** Assertive, Interrogative (Yes/No & Wh-), Imperative, Exclamatory, Optative.
* **Types of Sentences (Structure):** Simple Sentences, Compound Sentences, Complex Sentences.
* **Subject-Verb Agreement (Syntax):** 7+ Rules matching Subject with Verb.
* **Conditional Sentences:** Type 0, 1, 2, 3 (If clauses).
* **Clause Types:** Noun Clause, Adjective Clause, Adverb Clause.
* **Question Tags:** Positive & Negative tags, Exceptional rules.
* **Interchange / Transformation of Sentences:** 
  * Affirmative ↔ Negative.
  * Interchange of Degrees of Comparison.
  * Removal of "Too".
* **Synthesis of Sentences:** Combining simple sentences into compound/complex.

## 🟣 13. वाक्यों को घुमाना (Voice & Narration)
* **Active & Passive Voice:** Tense changes, Imperative sentences in passive, Prepositional passive, Agentless passive.
* **Direct & Indirect Speech (Narration):** Tense/Pronoun changes, Interrogative narration, Imperative/Exclamatory narration.

## 🟤 14. शब्दावली और मुहावरे (Vocabulary & Figures of Speech)

* **Phrasal Verbs:** Look up, Give up, Turn down.

* **Figures of Speech:** Simile, Metaphor, Personification, Alliteration.
