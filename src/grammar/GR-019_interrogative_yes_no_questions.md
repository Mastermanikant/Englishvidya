# Article 19 — Interrogative Sentences: Yes/No Questions (प्रश्नवाचक वाक्य — हाँ/ना)

### 🎯 Topic Introduction (विषय का परिचय)

क्या आपने कभी सोचा है कि हम अंग्रेज़ी में सवाल कैसे बनाते हैं? हिंदी में हम बस अपनी आवाज़ ऊपर उठा देते हैं — *"तुम खाना खाए?"* — और सवाल बन जाता है!

लेकिन अंग्रेज़ी में सवाल बनाने का एक **पक्का नियम** होता है — **Helping Verb (सहायक क्रिया) Subject से पहले आती है।**

इस Chapter में हम सीखेंगे **Yes/No Questions** — यानी वे सवाल जिनका जवाब सिर्फ **"Yes"** या **"No"** में दिया जा सकता है।

---

## ❓ Yes/No Question क्या है?

**Yes/No Question** वह सवाल है जिसका जवाब केवल **हाँ (Yes)** या **नहीं (No)** में दिया जा सकता है।

**Structure:** **Helping Verb + Subject + Main Verb + ?**

*(Declarative में Subject पहले था; Interrogative में Helping Verb पहले आती है — इसे "Inversion" कहते हैं।)*

---

## 🔄 कैसे बनाते हैं? — The Inversion Rule

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

**Declarative:** She **is** a teacher. (Subject पहले)  
**Interrogative:** **Is** she a teacher? (Helping Verb पहले)

### Step-by-Step:
1. Declarative वाक्य लिखें।
2. Helping Verb को Subject से पहले लाएँ (Inversion करें)।
3. अंत में **?** लगाएँ।

### उदाहरण:

| Declarative | Interrogative (Yes/No) |
|---|---|
| He is a doctor. | **Is** he a doctor? |
| She can swim. | **Can** she swim? |
| They are playing. | **Are** they playing? |
| You have eaten. | **Have** you eaten? |
| It was raining. | **Was** it raining? |

</details>

---

## 📋 Tense के हिसाब से नियम

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### 1. Present Simple (साधारण वर्तमान)
जब वाक्य में कोई Helping Verb नहीं होती (He goes. / She plays.) तो हम **Do/Does** जोड़ते हैं:
* **Do** + Subject (I/We/You/They) + V1 + ?
* **Does** + Subject (He/She/It) + V1 + ?

| Declarative | Yes/No Question |
|---|---|
| I like tea. | **Do** you like tea? |
| She plays guitar. | **Does** she play guitar? |
| They go to school. | **Do** they go to school? |

⚠️ **ध्यान दें:** Does के साथ Main Verb की 1st Form लगती है → ❌ Does she plays? → ✅ Does she play?

### 2. Past Simple (साधारण भूतकाल)
**Did** + Subject + V1 + ?

| Declarative | Yes/No Question |
|---|---|
| He went to market. | **Did** he go to market? |
| She cooked dinner. | **Did** she cook dinner? |

### 3. Future / Modals (भविष्यकाल / Modals)
Helping Verb (will, can, shall, could, would, should, may, might) + Subject + V1 + ?

| Declarative | Yes/No Question |
|---|---|
| He will come. | **Will** he come? |
| She can help. | **Can** she help? |
| You should rest. | **Should** you rest? |

### 4. Continuous Tenses (is/am/are/was/were + V-ing)
HV (is/am/are/was/were) + Subject + V-ing + ?

| Declarative | Yes/No Question |
|---|---|
| She is studying. | **Is** she studying? |
| They were playing. | **Were** they playing? |

### 5. Perfect Tenses (have/has/had + V3)
HV (have/has/had) + Subject + V3 + ?

| Declarative | Yes/No Question |
|---|---|
| He has eaten. | **Has** he eaten? |
| They had left. | **Had** they left? |

</details>

## 💬 Short Answers (छोटे उत्तर)

Yes/No Questions का जवाब पूरा वाक्य बोलने की ज़रूरत नहीं — Short Answer काफी है!

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

**Structure:**
* **Yes,** Subject + Helping Verb.
* **No,** Subject + Helping Verb + **not.**

### उदाहरण:
* *Is she a teacher?* → **Yes, she is.** / **No, she isn't.**
* *Do you like tea?* → **Yes, I do.** / **No, I don't.**
* *Did he go?* → **Yes, he did.** / **No, he didn't.**
* *Can she swim?* → **Yes, she can.** / **No, she can't.**
* *Have they eaten?* → **Yes, they have.** / **No, they haven't.**

⚠️ **ध्यान दें:** Short Answer में Main Verb नहीं दोहराते।
* ❌ *Yes, she is a teacher.* (इतना लंबा ज़रूरी नहीं)
* ✅ *Yes, she is.* (सही)

</details>

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes (आम गलतियाँ)

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### ❌ गलती 1: Inversion भूल जाना
* ❌ *She is coming?* (हिंदी स्टाइल में)
* ✅ *Is she coming?* (सही)

### ❌ गलती 2: Does के साथ V+s/es लगाना
* ❌ *Does she goes?*
* ✅ *Does she go?*

### ❌ गलती 3: Did के साथ V2 लगाना
* ❌ *Did he went?*
* ✅ *Did he go?*

</details>

## 🎯 Classroom Practice (कक्षा अभ्यास)

### अभ्यास 1: Declarative को Yes/No Question में बदलें
1. *She is happy.* → **Is she happy?**
2. *He plays cricket.* → **Does he play cricket?**
3. *They went to Delhi.* → **Did they go to Delhi?**
4. *You can drive.* → **Can you drive?**
5. *She has finished her work.* → **Has she finished her work?**

### अभ्यास 2: Short Answer दीजिए
1. *Are you a student?* → **Yes, I am.** / **No, I am not.**
2. *Did he call you?* → **Yes, he did.** / **No, he didn't.**

---

## 📌 Quick Revision (One-Liners)

* **Yes/No Question:** जिसका जवाब Yes या No में दे सकें।
* **Golden Rule:** Helping Verb + Subject + Main Verb + ?
* **No Helping Verb?** → Present: Do/Does + V1. Past: Did + V1.
* **Short Answer:** Yes/No + Subject + Helping Verb (only).
* **Common Error:** Does she *goes*? ❌ → Does she *go*? ✅
