# Article 35 — Use of Shall be / Will be: Interrogative Sentences

### 🎯 Topic Introduction

भविष्य के बारे में सवाल पूछने के लिए Will/Shall को Subject से पहले लाते हैं।

* क्या तुम कल यहाँ होगे? → ***Will** you be here tomorrow?*
* क्या यह आसान होगा? → ***Will** it be easy?*

---

## 📋 Interrogative Structure

**Yes/No:** Will/Shall + Subject + be + Noun/Adjective + ?
**Wh-:** Wh- Word + Will/Shall + Subject + be + ?

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### Yes/No Questions:
* **Will you be there?** (क्या तुम वहाँ होगे?)
* **Will she be happy?** (क्या वह खुश होगी?)
* **Will it be cold tomorrow?** (क्या कल ठंड होगी?)
* **Will they be ready by 6?** (क्या वे 6 बजे तक तैयार होंगे?)
* **Shall I be the first one?** (क्या मैं पहला होऊँगा?)

### Wh- Questions:
* **Where will you be next year?** (अगले साल तुम कहाँ होगे?)
* **What will the weather be like?** (मौसम कैसा होगा?)
* **Who will be the captain?** (कप्तान कौन होगा?)
* **How long will she be in hospital?** (वह कितने दिन अस्पताल में होगी?)

### Short Answers:
* *Will you be there?* → **Yes, I will.** / **No, I won't.**
* *Will she be ready?* → **Yes, she will.** / **No, she won't.**

</details>

## 📊 Complete Pattern Table — Am/Is/Are, Was/Were, Will/Shall be

|---|---|---|---|
| **Affirmative** | She is happy. | She was happy. | She will be happy. |
| **Negative** | She isn't happy. | She wasn't happy. | She won't be happy. |
| **Yes/No Q** | Is she happy? | Was she happy? | Will she be happy? |

## 🎯 Classroom Practice

### अभ्यास 1: Affirmative को Interrogative बनाएँ
1. *He will be a pilot.* → **Will he be a pilot?**
2. *They will be in Delhi.* → **Will they be in Delhi?**
3. *It will be sunny.* → **Will it be sunny?**

### अभ्यास 2: Wh- Questions बनाएँ
1. *(पूछना है — वह कल कहाँ होगा?)* → **Where will he be tomorrow?**
2. *(पूछना है — कप्तान कौन होगा?)* → **Who will be the captain?**
