# Article 31 — Use of Was, Were: Negative Sentences

### 🎯 Topic Introduction (विषय का परिचय)

जब हम भूतकाल में किसी बात को **नकारना** चाहते हैं — कि कोई ऐसा नहीं था, यह चीज़ ऐसी नहीं थी — तो Was/Were के साथ **'not'** जोड़ दिया जाता है।

* वह डरा हुआ **नहीं था।** → *He **was not** scared.*
* हम देर से **नहीं आए थे।** → *We **were not** late.*

---

## 📋 Negative Structure

**Structure:** Subject + was/were + **not** + Noun / Adjective / Prepositional Phrase

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### Contractions:
| Full Form | Contraction |
|---|---|
| was not | **wasn't** |
| were not | **weren't** |

### उदाहरण:
* **I was not at home.** / **I wasn't at home.** (मैं घर पर नहीं था।)
* **She was not angry.** / **She wasn't angry.** (वह गुस्से में नहीं थी।)
* **He was not a doctor.** / **He wasn't a doctor.** (वह डॉक्टर नहीं था।)
* **We were not ready.** / **We weren't ready.** (हम तैयार नहीं थे।)
* **They were not happy.** / **They weren't happy.** (वे खुश नहीं थे।)
* **The roads were not safe.** (सड़कें सुरक्षित नहीं थीं।)
* **The exam was not difficult.** (परीक्षा कठिन नहीं थी।)

</details>

## 🌟 Daily Life Examples

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

* **I wasn't feeling well yesterday.** (मैं कल ठीक नहीं था।)
* **She wasn't in school today.** (वह आज स्कूल में नहीं थी।)
* **They weren't my classmates.** (वे मेरे सहपाठी नहीं थे।)
* **The movie wasn't interesting.** (फ़िल्म दिलचस्प नहीं थी।)
* **We weren't late, I promise.** (हम देर से नहीं आए थे, मैं वादा करता हूँ।)

</details>

## 🔄 Affirmative से Negative बनाना

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

| Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|
| He was angry. | He **was not** / **wasn't** angry. |
| She was a teacher. | She **was not** / **wasn't** a teacher. |
| They were here. | They **were not** / **weren't** here. |
| I was wrong. | I **was not** / **wasn't** wrong. |
| The food was good. | The food **was not** / **wasn't** good. |

</details>

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### ❌ गलती 1: 'not' को Verb से पहले लगाना
* ❌ *She not was here.*
* ✅ *She **was not** here.*

### ❌ गलती 2: Present Tense का इस्तेमाल Past Events में
* ❌ *Yesterday, he isn't at home.*
* ✅ *Yesterday, he **wasn't** at home.*

</details>

## 🎯 Classroom Practice

### अभ्यास 1: Affirmative को Negative बनाएँ
1. *He was present.* → **He was not present.**
2. *We were late.* → **We were not late.**
3. *The test was easy.* → **The test wasn't easy.**
4. *I was nervous.* → **I wasn't nervous.**

### अभ्यास 2: हिंदी से अंग्रेज़ी
1. *वह डॉक्टर नहीं था।* → **He was not a doctor.**
2. *हम थके हुए नहीं थे।* → **We were not tired.**
3. *परीक्षा आसान नहीं थी।* → **The exam was not easy.**

---

## 📌 Quick Revision

* **Negative:** Subject + was/were + **not** + ...
* **wasn't** = was not | **weren't** = were not
* **'not' हमेशा was/were के बाद आता है।**
* **Yesterday, last year आदि के साथ → हमेशा was/were (not is/are)।**
