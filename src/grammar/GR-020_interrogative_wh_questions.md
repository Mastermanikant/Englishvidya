# Article 20 — Interrogative Sentences: Wh- Questions (प्रश्नवाचक वाक्य — Wh- शब्द)

### 🎯 Topic Introduction (विषय का परिचय)

पिछले Chapter में हमने **Yes/No Questions** सीखे। लेकिन अक्सर हमें सिर्फ हाँ या ना से ज़्यादा जानकारी चाहिए होती है:
* वह **कहाँ** गया? *(Where did he go?)*
* तुम **क्यों** रो रहे हो? *(Why are you crying?)*
* **कौन** आया था? *(Who came?)*

ऐसे सवालों के लिए हम **Wh- Words (प्रश्नवाचक शब्द)** का इस्तेमाल करते हैं। इनसे बने सवाल **Wh- Questions** कहलाते हैं, और इनका जवाब सिर्फ Yes/No में नहीं दिया जा सकता।

---

## 📚 Wh- Words का परिचय

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

| Wh- Word | हिंदी अर्थ | किसके लिए पूछते हैं? | उदाहरण |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What** | क्या | चीज़/काम/जानकारी | What is your name? |
| **Who** | कौन | व्यक्ति (Subject) | Who called you? |
| **Whom** | किसे/किसको | व्यक्ति (Object) | Whom did you call? |
| **Whose** | किसका/किसकी | स्वामित्व (Possession) | Whose book is this? |
| **Which** | कौन सा | चुनाव (Choice) | Which colour do you like? |
| **Where** | कहाँ | जगह (Place) | Where do you live? |
| **When** | कब | समय (Time) | When did she arrive? |
| **Why** | क्यों | कारण (Reason) | Why are you late? |
| **How** | कैसे/कितना | तरीका/मात्रा | How are you? |

### How के साथ कुछ ज़रूरी Combinations:
* **How many** — गिनने योग्य चीज़ों की संख्या *(How many apples?)*
* **How much** — बेशुमार चीज़ों की मात्रा *(How much water?)*
* **How long** — कितने समय तक *(How long did you wait?)*
* **How far** — कितनी दूर *(How far is the station?)*
* **How often** — कितनी बार *(How often do you exercise?)*
* **How old** — उम्र *(How old are you?)*

</details>

## 🔄 Wh- Question Structure

### Case 1: जब Wh- Word Object के बारे में पूछे

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

**Structure:** **Wh- Word + Helping Verb + Subject + Main Verb + ?**

*(यह Yes/No Question जैसा ही है — बस शुरुआत में Wh- Word जोड़ दो!)*

| Declarative | Yes/No Question | Wh- Question |
|---|---|---|
| She eats **rice**. | Does she eat rice? | **What** does she eat? |
| He went **to Delhi**. | Did he go to Delhi? | **Where** did he go? |
| They left **at 5 pm**. | Did they leave at 5 pm? | **When** did they leave? |
| She cried **because she was sad**. | Did she cry? | **Why** did she cry? |

**Step-by-Step:**
1. उस जानकारी को हटाएँ जिसके बारे में पूछना है।
2. उसकी जगह सही Wh- Word लगाएँ।
3. Inversion करें (HV को Subject से पहले लाएँ)।
4. **?** लगाएँ।

</details>

---

### Case 2: जब Wh- Word Subject के बारे में पूछे

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

यह बहुत ज़रूरी और दिलचस्प Case है। जब **Who, What, Which** खुद **Subject** की जगह आ जाएँ, तो **Inversion नहीं होती!**

**Structure:** **Who/What + Verb (3rd Person Singular) + ?**

### उदाहरण:
| Declarative | Subject Question |
|---|---|
| **Ram** called me. | **Who** called you? (not: Who did call you?) |
| **Something** happened. | **What** happened? (not: What did happen?) |
| **Five students** passed. | **How many students** passed? |

### क्यों Inversion नहीं होती?
क्योंकि Wh- Word खुद Subject है! Subject तो पहले से वाक्य में सबसे आगे होता है। इसलिए अलग से Inversion की ज़रूरत नहीं।

**तुलना देखें:**
* **Who did you call?** *(Who = Object — Inversion हुई: did + you)*
* **Who called you?** *(Who = Subject — कोई Inversion नहीं)*

</details>

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes (आम गलतियाँ)

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### ❌ गलती 1: Subject Question में Inversion करना
* ❌ *Who did call you?*
* ✅ *Who called you?*

### ❌ गलती 2: What/Where के साथ गलत Helping Verb
* ❌ *Where he went?*
* ✅ *Where did he go?*

### ❌ गलती 3: How much vs How many का confusion
* ❌ *How many water do you need?*
* ✅ *How much water do you need?* (water countable नहीं है)
* ✅ *How many bottles do you need?* (bottles countable है)

### ❌ गलती 4: Whom की जगह Who लगाना (Object में)
* *Whom did you meet?* ✅ (Formal — Object के लिए Whom)
* *Who did you meet?* ✅ (Informal — daily speech में Who चलता है)

</details>

## 🎯 Classroom Practice (कक्षा अभ्यास)

### अभ्यास 1: Underlined part के बारे में Wh- Question बनाएँ
1. *She bought **a new dress**.* → **What** did she buy?
2. *He lives **in Mumbai**.* → **Where** does he live?
3. *They left **because it was raining**.* → **Why** did they leave?
4. ***The principal** announced the result.* → **Who** announced the result?
5. *She called **her mother**.* → **Whom** did she call?
6. *He has **three** cats.* → **How many** cats does he have?

### अभ्यास 2: Subject या Object Question पहचानो
1. *Who broke the glass?* → **Subject Question** (Who = Subject)
2. *What did you see?* → **Object Question** (you = Subject, What = Object)
3. *Which team won?* → **Subject Question** (Which team = Subject)

---

## 📌 Quick Revision (One-Liners)

* **Wh- Question:** जिसका जवाब Yes/No से ज़्यादा हो।
* **Object Question:** Wh- Word + HV + Subject + V + ? *(Inversion होती है)*
* **Subject Question:** Who/What + V + ? *(Inversion नहीं होती)*
* **How combinations:** How many (countable), How much (uncountable), How long, How far, How often, How old.
* **Whom vs Who:** Whom = Object (Formal), Who = Object (Informal/daily speech).
