# Article 38 — Verb 'To Have' for Possession: Has / Have (वर्तमान मालिकाना हक)

### 🎯 Topic Introduction

जब हम यह बताना चाहते हैं कि **वर्तमान में किसी के पास कोई चीज़ है** (यानी किसी चीज़ पर मालिकाना हक है, या कोई रिश्ता/बीमारी है), तो हम **Has** या **Have** का प्रयोग करते हैं।

* मेरे पास एक कार **है।** → *I **have** a car.*
* उसके पास एक किताब **है।** → *He **has** a book.*
* मुझे बुखार **है।** → *I **have** a fever.*

---

## 📋 Has / Have — कौन किसके साथ?

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

| Subject | Verb | उदाहरण |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **have** | I have a pen. |
| **You** | **have** | You have time. |
| **We** | **have** | We have a house. |
| **They** | **have** | They have money. |
| **Plural Noun** | **have** | The boys have toys. |
| **He / She / It** | **has** | He has a car. / It has four legs. |
| **Singular Noun** | **has** | Ram has a book. |

**आसान नियम:**
* **I, You, We, They, Plural Nouns** → **have**
* **He, She, It, Singular Noun** → **has**

</details>

## ✅ Affirmative, Negative & Interrogative Structures

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### 1. Affirmative (सकारात्मक): Subject + Has/Have + Noun
* **I have a good idea.** (मेरे पास एक अच्छा विचार है।)
* **She has a sister.** (उसकी एक बहन है।)
* **A dog has four legs.** (कुत्ते के चार पैर होते हैं।)

### 2. Negative (नकारात्मक) — दो तरीके:

**तरीका A (Traditional/British): Subject + Has/Have + No/Not + Noun**
* *I have no money.* (मेरे पास पैसा नहीं है।)
* *He has no time.* (उसके पास समय नहीं है।)
* *I have not a single pen.* (मेरे पास एक भी पेन नहीं है।)

**तरीका B (Modern/American): Subject + do/does + not + have + Noun**
*(यह तरीका आज-कल ज़्यादा चलता है!)*
* *I **do not have** money.* (I don't have money.)
* *He **does not have** time.* (He doesn't have time.)

⚠️ **ध्यान दें:** Does के साथ हमेशा **'have'** आता है ('has' नहीं)!
* ❌ *He doesn't has a car.*
* ✅ *He doesn't have a car.*

### 3. Interrogative (प्रश्नवाचक) — दो तरीके:

**तरीका A:** Has/Have + Subject + Noun?
* *Have you a pen?*
* *Has she a car?*

**तरीका B:** Do/Does + Subject + have + Noun?
* ***Do** you **have** a pen?*
* ***Does** she **have** a car?*

</details>

## 🌟 Daily Life Examples

* **I have a headache.** (मुझे सिरदर्द है।)

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### ❌ गलती 1: Does के साथ Has लगाना
* ❌ *Does he has a pen?*
* ✅ *Does he **have** a pen?*

### ❌ गलती 2: I के साथ Has लगाना
* ❌ *I has a dog.*
* ✅ *I **have** a dog.*

## 🎯 Classroom Practice

### अभ्यास 1: Has या Have भरें
1. She ___ beautiful eyes. → **has**
2. I ___ two brothers. → **have**
3. The table ___ four legs. → **has**
4. They ___ a big house. → **have**

### अभ्यास 2: Negative (don't have / doesn't have) बनाएँ
1. *I have a car.* → **I don't have a car.**
2. *She has time.* → **She doesn't have time.**
3. *We have money.* → **We don't have money.**

---

## 📌 Quick Revision

* **Have:** I, You, We, They, Plural.
* **Has:** He, She, It, Singular.
* **Negative:** do not have / does not have.
* **Interrogative:** Do you have...? / Does she have...?
* **Rule of Thumb:** Does के बाद हमेशा **have** आता है।
