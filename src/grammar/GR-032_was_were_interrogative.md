# Article 32 — Use of Was, Were: Interrogative Sentences

### 🎯 Topic Introduction

भूतकाल में सवाल पूछने के लिए **Was/Were** को Subject से पहले लाते हैं।

* क्या वह घर पर था? → ***Was** he at home?*
* क्या वे तैयार थे? → ***Were** they ready?*

---

## 📋 Interrogative Structure

**Yes/No:** Was / Were + Subject + Noun/Adjective + ?
**Wh-:** Wh- Word + Was / Were + Subject + ?

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### Yes/No Questions:
* **Was she present?** (क्या वह उपस्थित थी?)
* **Was the food good?** (क्या खाना अच्छा था?)
* **Were they scared?** (क्या वे डरे हुए थे?)
* **Were you at home yesterday?** (क्या तुम कल घर पर थे?)
* **Was it a holiday?** (क्या वह छुट्टी का दिन था?)

### Wh- Questions:
* **Where were you last night?** (तुम कल रात कहाँ थे?)
* **Who was your teacher?** (तुम्हारे टीचर कौन थे?)
* **Why was she crying?** (वह क्यों रो रही थी?)
* **How was the movie?** (फ़िल्म कैसी थी?)
* **What was the problem?** (क्या समस्या थी?)

### Short Answers:
* *Was she a doctor?* → **Yes, she was.** / **No, she wasn't.**
* *Were they ready?* → **Yes, they were.** / **No, they weren't.**

</details>

## 🔄 Complete Pattern Table — Am/Is/Are vs Was/Were

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

| | Present (Am/Is/Are) | Past (Was/Were) |
|---|---|---|
| **Affirmative** | She is happy. | She was happy. |
| **Negative** | She is not happy. | She was not happy. |
| **Yes/No Q** | Is she happy? | Was she happy? |
| **Wh- Q** | Why is she happy? | Why was she happy? |

</details>

## 🎯 Classroom Practice

### अभ्यास 1: Affirmative को Interrogative बनाएँ
1. *She was a nurse.* → **Was she a nurse?**
2. *They were in the park.* → **Were they in the park?**
3. *He was angry.* → **Was he angry?**

### अभ्यास 2: Wh- Questions बनाएँ
1. *(पूछना है — वह कहाँ था?)* → **Where was he?**
2. *(पूछना है — परीक्षा कैसी थी?)* → **How was the exam?**
3. *(पूछना है — वे क्यों देर से थे?)* → **Why were they late?**

---

## 📌 Quick Revision

* **Was** + Singular Subject + ...? | **Were** + Plural/You + ...?
* **Short Answer:** Yes/No + Subject + was/were (only).
* **Inversion ज़रूरी:** Was/Were पहले, Subject बाद में।
