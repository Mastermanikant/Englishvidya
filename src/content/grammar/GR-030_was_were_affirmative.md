# Article 30 — Use of Was, Were: Affirmative Sentences

### 🎯 Topic Introduction (विषय का परिचय)

जब हम **बीते हुए समय** की किसी स्थिति, पहचान, या गुण के बारे में बात करते हैं — तो Am/Is/Are की जगह **Was/Were** का प्रयोग होता है। ये हिंदी के **"था, थी, थे"** के समान हैं।

* वह थका हुआ **था।** → *He **was** tired.*
* हम दोस्त **थे।** → *We **were** friends.*
* वह एक नेता **थी।** → *She **was** a leader.*

---

## 📋 Was / Were — कौन किसके साथ?

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

| Subject | Verb | उदाहरण |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | **was** | I was young. |
| **He / She / It** | **was** | He was a soldier. / It was cold. |
| **You** | **were** | You were right. |
| **We** | **were** | We were in Delhi. |
| **They** | **were** | They were my teachers. |
| **Singular Noun** | **was** | The door was open. |
| **Plural Noun** | **were** | The students were absent. |

**आसान याद करने का तरीका:**
* **I / He / She / It / Singular** → **was** (अकेला → was)
* **You / We / They / Plural** → **were** (समूह → were)

</details>

## ✅ Affirmative Examples

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### Was/Were + Adjective:
* **I was very happy yesterday.** (मैं कल बहुत खुश था।)
* **She was ill last week.** (वह पिछले हफ़्ते बीमार थी।)
* **They were nervous before the exam.** (परीक्षा से पहले वे घबराए हुए थे।)
* **The weather was pleasant.** (मौसम सुहावना था।)

### Was/Were + Noun:
* **He was a great king.** (वह एक महान राजा था।)
* **We were classmates in school.** (हम स्कूल में सहपाठी थे।)
* **She was a champion swimmer.** (वह एक चैंपियन तैराक थी।)

### Was/Were + Prepositional Phrase:
* **I was at home yesterday.** (मैं कल घर पर था।)
* **They were in Mumbai last month.** (वे पिछले महीने मुंबई में थे।)
* **The book was on the table.** (किताब मेज़ पर थी।)

</details>

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### ❌ गलती 1: I के साथ 'were' लगाना (Ordinary sentence में)
* ❌ *I were there.* (सामान्य वाक्य में गलत)
* ✅ *I **was** there.*
* *(अपवाद: "I wish I **were**..." — Subjunctive Mood में 'were' आता है — GR-023 देखें)*

### ❌ गलती 2: You के साथ 'was' लगाना
* ❌ *You was late.*
* ✅ *You **were** late.*

### ❌ गलती 3: Past की जगह Present Tense का उपयोग
* ❌ *Yesterday, he is at home.* (गलत — 'yesterday' Past दर्शाता है)
* ✅ *Yesterday, he **was** at home.*

</details>

## 🎯 Classroom Practice

### अभ्यास 1: सही Verb (was/were) भरें
1. I ___ very excited. → **was**
2. They ___ good friends. → **were**
3. She ___ a famous singer. → **was**
4. The children ___ in the park. → **were**
5. It ___ a rainy day. → **was**

### अभ्यास 2: हिंदी से अंग्रेज़ी
1. *वह बहुत बहादुर था।* → **He was very brave.**
2. *हम बहुत खुश थे।* → **We were very happy.**
3. *वह मेरी टीचर थीं।* → **She was my teacher.**

## 📌 Quick Revision

* **I/He/She/It/Singular → was** | **You/We/They/Plural → were**
* **था/थी → was** | **थे/थीं → were**
* **Past Time Markers:** yesterday, last week, last year, in 2020, once upon a time, ago आदि के साथ Was/Were।
* **अपवाद:** "I wish I were..." में Subjunctive के कारण I के साथ 'were' आता है।
