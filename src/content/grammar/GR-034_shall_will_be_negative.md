# Article 34 — Use of Shall be / Will be: Negative Sentences

### 🎯 Topic Introduction

भविष्य में किसी बात को नकारने के लिए will/shall के बाद **'not'** जोड़ते हैं।

* वे तैयार **नहीं होंगे।** → *They **will not be** ready.*
* मैं देर से **नहीं आऊँगा।** → *I **shall not be** late.*

---

## 📋 Negative Structure

**Structure:** Subject + will/shall + **not** + be + Noun/Adjective + ...

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### Contractions:
* will not → **won't**
* shall not → **shan't** (कम प्रयोग होता है)

### उदाहरण:
* **I will not be late.** / **I won't be late.** (मैं देर से नहीं आऊँगा।)
* **She will not be happy.** / **She won't be happy.** (वह खुश नहीं होगी।)
* **He will not be at home.** / **He won't be at home.** (वह घर पर नहीं होगा।)
* **We will not be ready in time.** (हम समय पर तैयार नहीं होंगे।)
* **They will not be available tomorrow.** (वे कल उपलब्ध नहीं होंगे।)
* **It will not be easy.** / **It won't be easy.** (यह आसान नहीं होगा।)

</details>

## 🔄 Affirmative → Negative

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

| Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|
| She will be a doctor. | She **will not be** a doctor. |
| They will be here. | They **won't be** here. |
| It will be cold. | It **won't be** cold. |
| I shall be ready. | I **shall not be** ready. |

</details>

---

## 🎯 Classroom Practice

### अभ्यास: Affirmative को Negative बनाएँ
1. *He will be free tomorrow.* → **He will not be free tomorrow.**
2. *She will be a great singer.* → **She won't be a great singer.**
3. *It will be rainy.* → **It won't be rainy.**
4. *We shall be late.* → **We shall not be late.**

---

## 📌 Quick Revision

* **Negative:** Subject + will/shall + **not** + be + ...
* **won't** = will not | **shan't** = shall not (rare)
* **'not' हमेशा will/shall के बाद, 'be' से पहले।**
