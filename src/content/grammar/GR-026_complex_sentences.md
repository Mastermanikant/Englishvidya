# Article 26 — Complex Sentences (मिश्रित वाक्य)

### 🎯 Topic Introduction (विषय का परिचय)

कभी-कभी हम एक काम का **कारण, समय, शर्त, या विरोध** बताना चाहते हैं। इसके लिए हमें दो Clauses को इस तरह जोड़ना होता है कि एक दूसरे पर निर्भर हो:

* *"I will go **if** it stops raining."* (मैं जाऊँगा, **अगर** बारिश बंद हो गई।)
* *"She cried **because** she was sad."* (वह रोई, **क्योंकि** वह दुखी थी।)
* ***"Although** he was tired, he kept working."* (**हालाँकि** वह थका था, वह काम करता रहा।)

ये **Complex Sentences (मिश्रित वाक्य)** हैं।

---

## 📌 Complex Sentence क्या है?

**Complex Sentence** वह वाक्य है जिसमें:
* एक **Independent Clause (Main Clause)** — जो अकेले पूरा अर्थ दे।
* एक या ज़्यादा **Dependent Clauses (Subordinate Clauses)** — जो अकेले अधूरे हों।

दोनों को **Subordinating Conjunctions** से जोड़ा जाता है।

**Structure A:** Independent Clause + Subordinating Conjunction + Dependent Clause
**Structure B:** Subordinating Conjunction + Dependent Clause **,** + Independent Clause

*(अगर Dependent Clause पहले आए तो comma लगता है!)*

---

## 🔗 Subordinating Conjunctions (अधीनस्थ समुच्चयबोधक)

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

| प्रकार | Conjunctions | उदाहरण |
|---|---|---|
| **Time (समय)** | when, while, before, after, since, until, as soon as | *I left **after** he arrived.* |
| **Reason (कारण)** | because, since, as | *She failed **because** she didn't study.* |
| **Condition (शर्त)** | if, unless, provided that, in case | *I'll help **if** you ask me.* |
| **Contrast (विरोध)** | although, though, even though, whereas | ***Although** it was cold, she went out.* |
| **Purpose (उद्देश्य)** | so that, in order that | *He works hard **so that** he can succeed.* |
| **Result (परिणाम)** | so...that, such...that | *He was **so** tired **that** he fell asleep.* |
| **Comparison** | than, as...as | *She is taller **than** I am.* |
| **Place (जगह)** | where, wherever | *I'll follow you **wherever** you go.* |

</details>

## ✅ Complex Sentences के उदाहरण

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### Dependent Clause अंत में:
* **She stayed home because she was sick.** (वह घर पर रही क्योंकि वह बीमार थी।)
* **I will call you when I reach.** (मैं तुम्हें फ़ोन करूँगा जब पहुँचूँ।)
* **He won't pass unless he works hard.** (वह पास नहीं होगा जब तक मेहनत न करे।)

### Dependent Clause शुरू में (Comma के साथ):
* **Because she was tired, she went to bed early.** (क्योंकि वह थकी थी, वह जल्दी सो गई।)
* **Although he tried hard, he failed.** (हालाँकि उसने बहुत कोशिश की, वह फेल हो गया।)
* **If it rains, we will cancel the match.** (अगर बारिश हुई, हम मैच रद्द कर देंगे।)

</details>

---

## 🆚 Simple vs Compound vs Complex — तीनों में फर्क

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

| | Simple | Compound | Complex |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clauses** | 1 Independent | 2+ Independent | 1 Independent + 1+ Dependent |
| **Conjunction** | नहीं | FANBOYS (and, but, so...) | Subordinating (because, if, when...) |
| **उदाहरण** | He worked hard. | He worked hard, **but** he failed. | He failed **because** he didn't work hard. |

### एक वाक्य — तीन रूप:
**Simple:** *Despite his hard work, he failed.*
**Compound:** *He worked hard, but he failed.*
**Complex:** *He failed because he didn't work hard enough.*

</details>

---

## 🔄 Transformation: Simple ↔ Complex

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

अक्सर परीक्षाओं में एक प्रकार के वाक्य को दूसरे प्रकार में बदलने के लिए कहा जाता है:

| Simple | Complex |
|---|---|
| *Being sick, she stayed home.* | *Because she was sick, she stayed home.* |
| *On seeing the dog, the cat ran.* | *When the cat saw the dog, it ran.* |
| *In spite of hard work, he failed.* | *Although he worked hard, he failed.* |
| *Too weak to walk, he sat down.* | *He was so weak that he couldn't walk.* |

</details>

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes (आम गलतियाँ)

<details>
  <summary>Click to see details (विस्तार से जानें)</summary>

### ❌ गलती 1: Comma भूलना जब Dependent Clause पहले हो
* ❌ *Although he was tired he worked.* (Comma नहीं)
* ✅ *Although he was tired**,** he worked.*

### ❌ गलती 2: दो Subordinating Conjunctions एक साथ लगाना
* ❌ *Although he tried, but he failed.* (although और but एक साथ नहीं)
* ✅ *Although he tried, he failed.* **OR** *He tried, but he failed.*

### ❌ गलती 3: Unless के साथ 'not' लगाना
* ❌ *Unless you don't study, you will fail.*
* ✅ *Unless you study, you will fail.* (unless में already 'not' का मतलब है)

</details>

## 🎯 Classroom Practice (कक्षा अभ्यास)

### अभ्यास 1: Simple, Compound, या Complex?
1. **I left when he arrived.** → Complex (*when* = Subordinating Conjunction)
2. **She studied, so she passed.** → Compound (*so* = FANBOYS)
3. **The tall girl in blue dress sang.** → Simple (एक Clause)
4. **Although it was late, he kept working.** → Complex

### अभ्यास 2: जोड़ो सही Conjunction से
1. *I will not go. You ask me.* → **I will not go unless you ask me.**
2. *She left. The movie ended.* → **She left after the movie ended.**
3. *He studied hard. He wanted to pass.* → **He studied hard so that he could pass.**

---

## 📌 Quick Revision (One-Liners)

* **Complex Sentence:** 1 Independent + 1+ Dependent Clause, Subordinating Conjunction से जुड़े।
* **Dependent Clause पहले हो** → Comma लगाओ।
* **Although + but** → एक साथ नहीं! सिर्फ एक choose करो।
* **Unless** = If...not (unless के साथ 'not' मत लगाओ)।
* **Key Test:** Conjunction हटाओ — अगर एक हिस्सा अधूरा लगे → वह Dependent Clause है → Complex Sentence!
