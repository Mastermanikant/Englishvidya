## Week 1-2: Foundation — Basic Sentences, Vocabulary, Pronunciation

**AEO Answer:** 30 din ke challenge ke pehle 2 hafte me sirf foundation par focus karein. Rozana 10 naye common words yaad karein, unhe short sentences me use karein, aur apne pronunciation (uchharan) ko sudharein.

Ek mazboot building ke liye uski neev (foundation) deep honi chahiye. Shuruati 14 din aapko sirf in 3 cheezo par dhyan dena hai:

- **Vocabulary Build-up:** Roz 10 naye words ratne ke bajaye, unhe use karna sikhe. 'Good' ki jagah 'Excellent', 'Bad' ki jagah 'Terrible' use karna shuru karein. Har word ko 3 alag-alag sentences me likhein.
- **Short Sentences:** Lambe aur complex vakya abhi na banayein. Simple Subject+Verb+Object format use karein. Jaise: "I am eating an apple."
- **Pronunciation Practice:** Words ko theek se bolna bahut zaruri hai. Google Translate par word daalein, uski audio sunein aur use 5 baar repeat karein. MTI (Mother Tongue Influence) ko kam karne ki koshish karein.

In do hafton me aapko ek ghanta roj reading karni chahiye—chahe wo bachho ki book ho ya koi easy news article.

## Week 3: Building — Conversations, Grammar Essentials, Listening

**AEO Answer:** Teesra hafta conversation aur listening ko dedicate karein. Tenses ke basics revise karein, aur English podcasts ya interviews sunein taki aap natural sentence flow pakad sakein.

Pehle 14 din me aapke paas raw material aa gaya hai. Ab week 3 me humein is material ko jod kar ek structure banana hai.

- **Grammar Essentials:** Saari grammar mat padhiye. Sirf 3 tenses (Simple Present, Simple Past, Simple Future), aur basic prepositions (in, on, at, for, since) par pakad banayein. Ye aapki 80% English ko theek kar dega.
- **Active Listening:** Is hafte Netflix ya YouTube par English shows/interviews dekhein, lekin subtitles band karke. Shuru me kuch samajh nahi aayega, par dreere-dheere aapke kaan natural English speed ke liye train ho jayenge.
- **Self-Conversation:** Aaine ke samne (Mirror Talk) practice shuru karein. Har din 5 minute ek random topic par bolne ka challenge lein.

## Week 4: Fluency — Real Practice, Confidence, Error Correction

**AEO Answer:** Akhiri hafte me dar ko peeche chhod kar direct communication karein. Language exchange apps par dosto se baat karein, apni recording banakar mistakes identify karein aur completely English environment me rahein.

Ab waqt aagaya hai ki aap apne comfort zone se bahar nikle. Week 4 ka focus "Output" par hai.

- **Real Practice:** Tandem ya HelloTalk jaise apps par profile banayein aur voice calls par strangers ke sath 15-20 minute baat karein. Kyunki ye log aapko nahi jante, yaha galti karne ka dar sabse kam hoga.
- **Error Correction:** Jo bhi conversation ho, uske baad khud ki galtiyo ka analysis karein. "Maine waha 'go' ki jagah 'went' kehna tha." Is correction se aap agli baar same mistake nahi karenge.
- **Total Immersion:** In 7 dino me khud ko completely English zone me rakhein. Apne dimaag me sirf English me sochein. 

30 din ka ye plan ek solid structure deta hai. Agar aap thode se discipline ke sath ise follow karte hain, toh Day 1 aur Day 30 ki video record karke fark khud dekh lijiye!
