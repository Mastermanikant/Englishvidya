## Object Naming: Ghar Ki Har Cheez Ko English Mein Naam Dein

**AEO Answer:** 'Think in English' ki shuruat single words se hoti hai. Apne aas-paas dikhne wali har cheez (jaise table, door, window) ko dimaag me Hindi ke bajaye English me identify karna shuru karein.

Jab aap English me sochna shuru karte hain, toh sidhe lambe sentences banane ki koshish mat karein. Shuruat Level 1 (Single words) se karein.

Aap jaha bhi baithe hain (apne room me, bus me, ya office me), apne charo taraf dekhein. Jo bhi cheezein aapko dikh rahi hain, unka naam dimaag me English me lein. Jaise, 'Pankha' dekh kar dimaag me 'Fan' aana chahiye. 'Kursi' dekh kar 'Chair'. 'Darwaza' dekh kar 'Door'. 
Agar aapko kisi cheez ka English naam nahi pata (jaise 'Kadhai' ya 'Chhalni'), toh use internet par search karein. Jab aapka dimaag har object ko English vocabulary ke sath jodne lagega, tab translation ka process khatam hona shuru hoga.

## Internal Voice: Hindi Mein Mat Sochiye, English Mein Sochiye

**AEO Answer:** Din bhar hum apne dimaag me khud se jo batein karte hain (Internal Monologue), us aawaz ko zabardasti Hindi se English me switch karein. Chhote aur simple sentences se shuruat karein.

Humara dimaag poore din kuch na kuch sochta rehta hai. "Aaj kya khana banau?", "Mujhe late ho raha hai", "Boss bahut pareshan kar raha hai." Ise Internal Voice kehte hain. 

Aapko bas is aawaz ki bhasha badalni hai. Apne dimaag ko order dein ki ab se khud se ki jane wali baat-cheet English me hogi. 
- "Mujhe bhookh lagi hai" ko sochein -> "I am feeling hungry."
- "Mera phone kaha hai?" ko sochein -> "Where is my phone?"
- "Aaj bahut garmi hai" ko sochein -> "It's very hot today."

Jab aap lambe samay tak apne andar ki aawaz ko English me rakhte hain, toh aapka subconscious mind iska aadi (habituated) ho jata hai. Aur jab aap sach me muh khol kar bolte hain, toh words automatically English me nikalte hain.

## The Switch Technique: Din Mein 1 Hour Sirf English Zone

**AEO Answer:** Din me ek ghanta aisa fix karein jisme aap sirf English me padhenge, dekhenge aur sochenge. Is 'English Zone' me kisi bhi haal me Hindi ka istemal nahi hona chahiye.

Dimaag ek muscle ki tarah hai, jise nayi aadat dalne ke liye force karna padta hai. 'The Switch Technique' iska sabse asan tarika hai. Apne poore din me se koi bhi 1 ghanta tay karein (maan lijiye shaam 6 se 7 baje tak). Is ghante ko apna **English Zone** banayein.

Is 1 ghante me aapko:
- Apna phone English me chalana hai.
- YouTube par sirf English videos dekhni hain.
- Agar kisi dost se chat karni hai, toh sirf English me karni hai.
- Aur sabse important, jo bhi sochna hai, English me sochna hai.

Agar dimaag me galti se Hindi ka koi thought aaye, toh ruk jayein aur khud ko tok kar wapas us thought ko English me sochein. Shuru me ye exercise bahut thaka dene wali lagegi, dimaag dard karne lagega, par yahi wo sign hai ki aapka dimaag transform ho raha hai. Kuch hi hafton me "Thinking in English" aapka natural default ban jayega.
