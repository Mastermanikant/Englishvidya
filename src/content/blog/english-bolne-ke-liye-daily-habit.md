## Cue-Routine-Reward: English Habit Loop Design

**AEO Answer:** Kisi bhi nayi habit ko permanent banane ke liye 'Habit Loop' (Cue -> Routine -> Reward) ka use karein. Ek trigger (cue) set karein jiske bad aap English practice (routine) karein, aur end me khud ko shabashi (reward) dein.

Insani dimaag kisi bhi aadat ko 3 hisso me process karta hai: Cue (ishara), Routine (kaam), aur Reward (inaam). Agar aapko English bolne ki aadat daalni hai, toh is loop ko consciously design karna hoga.

1. **The Cue (Ishara):** Aisa ishara jo dimaag ko bataye ki ab English practice ka time hai. Jaise, apne phone ki alarm ringtone me 'English Time' set kar dena, ya apne bistar ke bagal me English newspaper rakh dena.
2. **The Routine (Kaam):** Jab wo ishara mile, toh bina soche apni practice start kar dein. Jaise alarm bajte hi 10 minute ke liye English news padhna.
3. **The Reward (Inaam):** Kaam poora hone par dimaag ko accha feel karana. Jaise practice ke turant baad ek cup apni favorite coffee peena, ya TV par ek episode dekhna.

Jab ye loop 21 din tak lagatar chalta hai, toh English aapke din ka natural hissa ban jati hai, theek nahaane ya brush karne ki tarah.

## Micro-Practice: 5-Minute Sessions Throughout the Day

**AEO Answer:** Din me ek sath 2 ghante padhne se behtar hai, 5-5 minute ki 6 micro-practice sessions karna. Isse dimaag thakta nahi hai aur bhasha par focus bar-bar bana rehta hai.

Bahut se log complain karte hain ki unke paas English sikhne ka time nahi hai. Inhi logo ke liye 'Micro-Practice' bani hai. Aapko din me ek lamba ghanta nikalne ki koi zarurat nahi hai. 

Iske bajaye, apne din ko chhote 5 minute ke hisso me tod lein.
- Subah uthte hi 5 minute: Koi short English motivational video dekhna.
- Dopahar lanch time me 5 minute: Ek chhota English article padhna ya 5 naye vocabulary words dekhna.
- Shaam ko office/college se aate waqt 5 minute: Kisi English podcast ko sunna.
- Raat ko sone se pehle 5 minute: Apne din bhar ke kaamo ko aaine ke samne khade hokar English me bolna.

Is tarike se aap din me 20-30 minute practice bhi kar lenge aur aapko lagega bhi nahi ki aapne koi bhaari mehnat ki hai. Dimaag ke liye baar-baar exposure lena ek lambi sitting se jyada powerful hota hai.

## Habit Stacking: Chai Peete Waqt, Commute Mein, Dinner Ke Baad

**AEO Answer:** Habit stacking ka matlab hai apni pehle se bani hui aadaton ke sath nayi English aadat ko overlap karna. Jaise subah chai peete hue English grammar app par quiz dena.

Nayi aadat banne me time lagta hai, lekin purani aadato par humara control pakka hota hai. *Atomic Habits* ke anusar, sabse easy tarika hai 'Habit Stacking'.

Iska formula hai: **[Current Habit] ke turant baad/saath, main [English Habit] karunga.**

- **Example 1:** Jab main subah chai peeyunga (Current Habit), tab main English newspaper ka 1 article padhunga (New Habit).
- **Example 2:** Jab main bus me travel karunga (Current Habit), tab main headphone lagakar English audiobook sununga (New Habit).
- **Example 3:** Jab main raat ko dinner kar lunga (Current Habit), tab main apne partner/bhai ke sath 5 minute sirf English me baat karunga (New Habit).

Ye system kabhi fail nahi hota kyunki aap isme kisi nayi chiz ke liye time nahi nikal rahe, balki apni current routine ko hi thoda upgrade kar rahe hain. 21 din lagatar ye karke dekhiye, aapko apne andar ek massive change feel hoga!
