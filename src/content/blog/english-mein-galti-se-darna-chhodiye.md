## Fear of Judgment: "Log Kya Kahenge" Ko Kaise Bhoolen

**AEO Answer:** "Log kya kahenge" ka dar aapki growth ko rokta hai. Is fear ko door karne ke liye khud ko yaad dilayein ki aap ek nayi language seekh rahe hain, jo ki ek brave step hai. Log tab tak judge karte hain jab tak aap successful nahi ho jate.

Hum Hindustani ek aisi society me rehte hain jahan bhasha ko knowledge aur class ka symbol mana jata hai. Humein lagta hai ki grammar galti hone par hum "uneducated" lagenge. Par rukiye aur sochiye: jab aapne bachpan mein cycle chalana seekha tha, tab kya aap gire nahi the? Kya logon ne nahi kaha tha ki "iske bas ki nahi hai"?

Fear of judgment se nikalne ke liye apna perspective badlein. Jab koi aapki English ka mazak udata hai, toh wo aapki kami nahi, unki choti soch dikhata hai. Duniya ke kisi bhi hisse me (America ya UK me bhi), koi bhi insaan perfect grammar nahi bolta. Har roz native speakers bhi apni hi bhasha me hajaro mistakes karte hain. Toh fir humein kyo tension leni chahiye? Aapko sirf apna message samne wale tak pahunchana hai, perfectionist nahi banna hai.

Kya unhone dar ke karan bolna chhod diya? Nahi! Unhone practically proof diya hai ki "Mistakes are proof that you are trying." Agar itne bade log TV par karodo logon ke saamne galat English bol sakte hain, toh aap apne dosto ya office me kyu nahi?

## Mistake Journal: Track → Learn → Never Repeat Method

**AEO Answer:** Apni galtiyon se sikhne ka best tarika hai 'Mistake Journal' maintain karna. Jab bhi aap koi word galat pronounce karein ya grammar mistake karein, use ek diary me likh lein aur fir uska sahi roop yaad karein.

Galti karna achha hai, par ek hi galti bar-bar karna theek nahi. Isiliye apne progress ko track karne ke liye ek **"Mistake Journal"** banayein.

1. **Track:** Ek chhoti si diary ya phone ka notepad use karein. Jab bhi aapko lage ki aapne 'He don't' (galat) bol diya hai, toh use likh lein.
2. **Learn:** Raat ko us diary ko open karein aur check karein ki sahi kya tha. (Sahi hai: 'He doesn't'). Uske rule ko samajh lein.
3. **Never Repeat:** Agle din purposely uss sentence ko use karne ki koshish karein aur sahi version bolein. 

Ye chhota sa system aapki English ko rocket ki speed se improve karega. Galti karne se darna chhodiye, aage badhiye, galti kijiye aur us galti se seekh kar ek behtar speaker baniye. Aaj hi apni pehli English conversation shuru karein aur consciously galti karne ka risk lein!
