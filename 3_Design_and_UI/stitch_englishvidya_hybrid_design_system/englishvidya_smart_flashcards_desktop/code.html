Resilience

/rɪˈzɪliəns/
Click to flip
Noun

The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties; toughness.

"The resilience of the students during exam season was inspiring."

परीक्षा के मौसम के दौरान छात्रों का लचीलापन प्रेरणादायक था।

Synonyms: toughness, flexibility Antonyms: vulnerability, weakness
